Cookie Policy

This policy explains how LASTGAZE uses cookies and similar technologies on our website, and how you can control them.

What Cookies Are

Cookies are small text files placed on your device — computer, phone, or tablet — when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work correctly, to remember your preferences, and to provide website operators with basic information about how their sites are being used.

Cookies do not identify you personally. They identify your browser or device, and they are read only by the site that set them.

Why We Use Cookies

We use cookies to make the LASTGAZE website function as expected, to remember certain preferences during your visit, and to understand in aggregate how visitors use the site. We do not use cookies to track individuals across other websites, and we do not use them to serve advertising.

Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to function. They enable core features such as security, session continuity, and page navigation. Without them, the site cannot operate correctly. Essential cookies cannot be disabled through our settings — though you can always clear them through your browser.

Analytics Cookies

Analytics cookies help us understand how visitors interact with the LASTGAZE website. They tell us which pages are visited most often, how long people spend, and where they arrive from. This information is collected and reviewed in aggregate — it does not identify individual visitors. We use it to understand what is working and to improve the site over time.

Preference Cookies

Preference cookies remember choices you make during your visit — such as items you have added to your cart — so that you do not need to re-enter them when you navigate between pages or return to the site. These cookies expire after a reasonable period of time.

Managing Cookies Through Your Browser

You can control, limit, or delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to view the cookies stored on your device, delete some or all of them, block cookies from specific sites, and block all cookies by default.

Be aware that disabling cookies may affect how the LASTGAZE website functions. Essential cookies in particular are required for the site to operate as intended.

You can find instructions for managing cookies in the most common browsers here:

Chrome — Settings > Privacy and Security > Cookies and other site data
Firefox — Settings > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data
Safari — Preferences > Privacy > Manage Website Data
Edge — Settings > Cookies and site permissions

Updates

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in how we use cookies or in applicable requirements. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised date.
