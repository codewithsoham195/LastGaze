How Drops Work

Vintage drops in India usually mean a vague "restock soon." Ours are a fixed weekly ritual — here is exactly how it works.

Every Saturday, eight pieces

A new drop goes live every Saturday. Each drop is a small, fixed set of pieces — we do not pad it out to look bigger than it is. When you see a lot number on a listing, that is literally the order it was found and catalogued in.

One of one, no restocks

Every piece is a single physical garment. There is no second unit, no "back in stock" notification, and no reprint. If a size or a piece is gone, it is gone — that scarcity is not a marketing device, it is just how vintage sourcing works.

Once a piece sells, it does not disappear. It moves into the archive, where it stays listed permanently as a record of what has passed through LASTGAZE.

Condition, graded honestly

Every listing carries a condition score out of 10 and a plain description of any wear — fading, repairs, frayed hems, the works. We would rather under-promise on condition than have you unbox something you did not expect.

Measurements are listed in inches on every piece — chest, length, shoulder, and sleeve for tops; waist, inseam, rise, and leg opening for denim. Vintage sizing does not track modern S/M/L, so we always measure. See the sizing guide for how to compare your own numbers to a listing.

How to actually get a piece

Drops open at a fixed time on Saturday and pieces go on a first-come basis — there is no waitlist or lottery. The fastest way to know the moment a drop opens is to sign up for updates; we send a note before each release, not after.

After you buy

Order status, saved addresses, and past purchases live in your account. Shipping and returns are covered in full under Terms & Conditions.
