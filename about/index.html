About

LASTGAZE is a vintage menswear store and archive, working out of New Delhi. Eight pieces a week. Nothing restocks.

A vintage store, not a warehouse

We are a small vintage store based in Delhi, not a marketplace and not a reseller network. Everything that reaches the site has been found, inspected, and measured by hand, close to home, before it is ever listed.

We are not trying to look like a big brand. We are trying to be a reliable one — the kind of vintage store in Delhi where a returning customer already trusts what "8.5/10 condition" means, because we have never rounded up.

How we source

Every piece comes from the same place a vintage archive should come from — wardrobes, markets, and estates around Delhi, sorted by hand. We are not importing container loads of stock. We are looking for construction, fabric, and condition worth putting a name to.

Most of what we find does not make the site. What does is graded, measured, and photographed before it is listed — see how the weekly drop works for the exact process.

One of one

Every lot on LASTGAZE is a single physical garment. There is no second unit in a back room. Once it sells, it moves to the archive and stays there — a public record of everything that has passed through, permanently.
