The Archive

A vintage archive is only as good as its record-keeping. Every one-of-one piece that has ever sold through LASTGAZE stays listed here, permanently — nothing gets quietly deleted once it's gone.

Why we keep this page

Most vintage stores in India show you what's for sale and nothing else — once a piece sells, it vanishes without a trace. We think the sold history is part of the point. It shows what we actually source, drop after drop, not just what happens to be in stock this week.
